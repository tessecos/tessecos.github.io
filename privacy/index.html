PRIVACY隐私

隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-21 · 适用于《归序》（Tessecos）1.0.3 及之后版本

无需创建游戏账号，核心玩法离线运行。

存档保存在当前设备，不向开发者上传，也不通过云端同步。

Apple 负责购买与恢复购买；游戏使用 Google AdMob 提供可选激励广告、底部横幅及关卡间插屏广告。

1. 开发团队与适用范围

本政策说明由 Tessecos Studio 独立开发团队维护的 《归序》（Tessecos）游戏及本网站如何处理数据。隐私问题可通过下方支持邮箱联系。

2. 设备中的游戏数据

关卡、棋盘与操作历史、用时、生成地图、通关记录、道具数量、主题和语言、声音、外观偏好保存在应用的本地目录。开发者无法远程读取这些存档。

3. Apple 内购服务

游戏使用 Apple StoreKit 2 查询商品、核验单套皮肤及“全部皮肤 + 去广告”的购买权益，并恢复购买。这些均为一次性非消耗型商品，不是订阅。Apple 处理支付与交易；游戏不会读取 Apple 密码或银行卡信息，只接收商品信息与核验结果。权益不通过云端保存的购买标记授予。

Apple 服务适用 Apple 隐私政策。

4. 广告与权限

游戏提供主动观看激励广告获取当局道具的入口，并可在游戏画面技能栏下方显示横幅。普通与困难共用实际游玩计时，每累计八分钟，仅在通关后进入下一关时请求插屏；菜单、弹窗、广告和后台时间不计入。点击有效激励广告入口即重新计时，插屏不可用或展示失败时跳过本次并重新计时。经核验购买“全部皮肤 + 去广告”后移除横幅、插屏和激励广告入口。广告由 Google AdMob 提供，需要时预先加载。广告服务可能处理 IP 地址及由此推断的大致位置、设备或广告标识、广告展示和使用交互、崩溃和性能诊断数据；详见 Google 隐私政策及 Google SDK 数据披露。需要时由 Google 的隐私消息收集选择。游戏不请求相机、麦克风、定位或通讯录权限。广告不可用或未获得 SDK 领奖确认时不发奖励。当地要求时，游戏帮助中心显示“广告隐私选择”，可重新打开并修改选择。

iOS 安装归因

启用此功能的 iOS 版本使用 Tenjin 衡量广告是否带来了《归序》的安装。游戏在适用的隐私同意要求得到满足、且你通过 Apple 的“App 跟踪透明度”授权后，才会向 Tenjin 发送应用打开、广告展示收入和通关事件。通关事件仅发送固定关卡编号或生成关卡的通关标记，不发送存档、棋盘或尝试标识。广告展示收入事件可包含广告单元、展示响应标识、广告来源、金额、币种和金额精度。Tenjin 可能处理广告或设备标识符、IP 地址及由此推断的大致地区、设备型号、系统和应用版本，以及用于归因的广告点击和展示信息。Tenjin 用广告展示收入衡量各获客渠道的表现。Tenjin 仅向 AppLovin 发送归因到 AppLovin 广告活动的安装回传，供 AppLovin 衡量广告效果。

你可拒绝跟踪并继续游玩，也可在 iOS 的“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪”中更改授权。当游戏显示“广告隐私选择”时，你也可以在那里更新适用的同意选项。你可通过下方邮箱提出数据访问或删除请求。更多信息见 Tenjin 隐私政策和 AppLovin 隐私政策。

5. 保存与恢复

进度和道具保存在本机，没有跨设备存档同步。删除应用及其数据可能丢失进度；系统备份是否可恢复取决于设备设置。广告获得的道具绑定当前对局，重开或换关失效，继续同一存档局面仍有效。

单套皮肤和“全部皮肤 + 去广告”权益可使用原购买 Apple 账号在商店点击“恢复购买”核验；恢复权益不会恢复本机棋盘或道具记录。

6. 支持邮件

如果你主动发送支持邮件，运营者会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容及你主动附上的截图或设备信息，仅用于回复和处理请求。请勿发送密码、完整付款凭证或其他不必要的敏感信息。支持邮件仅在处理问题及适用要求所需的期间保留；你可以通过支持邮箱提出访问或删除请求。

7. 本网站与外部链接

本网站是静态页面，没有广告、分析脚本、登录系统或在线表单。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管；GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者 IP 等技术信息，具体请参阅 GitHub Pages 数据说明和 GitHub 隐私声明。

访问 Apple、GitHub 等外部网站时，相应网站和浏览器会按各自政策处理访问数据。

8. 政策更新

若应用的数据处理方式发生变化，将更新本政策及必要的商店隐私说明，并标明更新日期。

Privacy policy

Updated: 2026-11-21 · Applies to Tessecos 1.0.3 and later

No game account is required. Core gameplay works offline.

Saves stay on the current device and are neither uploaded to the developer nor synced through the cloud.

Apple handles purchases and restoration. The game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads, bottom banners and interstitials between levels.

1. Developer team and scope

This policy describes data handling for Tessecos and this support website, maintained by the independent team Tessecos Studio. Contact the support email below for privacy questions.

2. Local game data

Levels, boards and action history, elapsed time, generated maps, completion records, tool counts, themes, and language, audio and appearance preferences stay in the app’s local directory. The developer cannot remotely read these saves.

3. Apple purchases

Apple StoreKit 2 queries products, verifies ownership and restores individual skins and the All Skins + No Ads bundle. These are one-time non-consumable purchases, not subscriptions. Apple handles payments and transactions. The game does not read Apple passwords or payment-card details; it receives product information and verified results. Cloud purchase flags never grant ownership.

Apple services follow the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising and permissions

The game provides optional rewarded-ad buttons for tools that last for the current run and may display a banner below the tools during gameplay. Normal and Hard share an active-play clock: after eight minutes, an interstitial is requested only when continuing to the next level after a win. Menus, dialogs, ads and background time do not count. Tapping a valid rewarded-ad entry resets this clock. Unavailable or failed interstitials are skipped and start a new interval. Verified ownership of All Skins + No Ads removes banners, interstitials and rewarded-ad buttons. Ads may be preloaded before the level transition. The service may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or advertising identifiers, ad views and usage interactions, crash reports and performance diagnostics; see the Google Privacy Policy and Google SDK data disclosure. Google privacy messages collect choices when required. The game requests no camera, microphone, location or contacts access. Unavailable ads or ads without an SDK-earned reward grant nothing. When required in your region, Help shows Ad Privacy Choices so you can reopen and change your choices.

iOS install attribution

iOS versions with this feature use Tenjin to measure whether ads led to Tessecos installs. The game sends app-open, ad impression revenue, and level completion events to Tenjin only after applicable privacy consent requirements are met and you allow tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. Level completion events identify the numbered level or mark a generated level as completed. Game saves, boards, and attempt identifiers stay on the device. Ad impression revenue events may include the ad unit, impression response identifier, ad source, amount, currency and reporting precision. Tenjin may process advertising or device identifiers, IP addresses and approximate regions inferred from them, device models, operating system and app versions, and ad click and impression data used for attribution. Tenjin uses ad impression revenue to measure performance by acquisition channel. Tenjin sends install postbacks to AppLovin only for installs attributed to AppLovin campaigns. AppLovin uses this data to measure ad performance.

You can decline tracking and keep playing. Change your permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If the game shows Ad Privacy Choices, you can update the applicable consent choices there. You can use the support email below to request access to or deletion of your data. See the Tenjin Privacy Policy. For more information about AppLovin’s collection and use of your information, visit its Privacy Policy.

5. Saves and recovery

Progress and tools stay on the device, with no cross-device save sync. Deleting the app and its data may lose progress; system backup recovery depends on your device settings. Ad tools belong to the current run, expire on restart or level change, and remain valid when continuing the same saved run.

Restore Purchases in the shop can verify individual skins and All Skins + No Ads with the original purchasing Apple Account. Restoring ownership does not recover local boards or tool records.

6. Support email

If you email support, the operator receives your email address, message and any screenshots or device information you choose to include, solely to respond and handle your request. Do not send passwords, full payment records or unnecessary sensitive information. Support messages are kept only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable requirements. Contact support to request access or deletion.

7. This website and external links

This is a static website with no ads, analytics scripts, sign-in system or web forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses and technical information for security; see GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you visit external Apple or GitHub pages, those websites and your browser process visits under their own policies.

8. Changes

If data handling changes, this policy and necessary App Store privacy disclosures will be updated, with a revised date.
