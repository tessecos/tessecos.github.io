玩家支持

“最少”怎么算？

只数完整出现在屏幕里的水果；正在从上方进入或从下方离开的半透明水果不计数，也不能点击。数量最少的那一种都可以点；几种并列最少时，任选一种都对。只剩一种水果时，它就是最少的。

点错了会怎样？

每关有 3 点生命，点错扣 1 点并短暂停顿，生命用完这一关就失败，可以马上重试。护盾可以挡住一次点错。没点到的水果落出屏幕后会从上方重新出现，不扣分，清空全部水果才算通关。

技能水果是什么？

技能水果带有彩色光晕，随时点击都不算点错，没点到也不扣分。第 10、20、30、40、50 关依次解锁闪电葡萄（提示最少的水果）、爆炸石榴（炸掉周围的水果）、冰镇西瓜（让水果变慢）、爱心草莓（恢复生命）和流星杨桃（自动消掉最少的水果）；之后每 10 关升级一种技能。每组第 10 关是技能狂欢关，技能水果会一直出现。

水果为什么有时快、有时慢？

每关有固定的起始速度，消除得越多，水果下落越快。玩满 20 局后，游戏会参考最近 20 局的胜率和连胜、连败情况微调速度：打得顺会稍快，连续失败会稍慢，每局变化很小，不会突然变化。

可以回到以前的关卡吗？

可以。在开始、暂停或结算页点击“选关”，或在“设置 → 选择关卡”中，选择任意已经到达的关卡。

怎样重新开始？换设备后进度会同步吗？

在“设置 → 清除进度”中可以清除全部进度与成绩，从新手教学重新开始，此操作无法恢复。进度只保存在当前设备，没有跨设备同步；删除应用会丢失进度，系统备份能否恢复取决于设备设置。

如何反馈问题？

Player support

What counts as “fewest”?

Only fruit fully on the screen count. Half-transparent fruit coming in at the top or leaving at the bottom are not counted and cannot be tapped. Tap any fruit of the kind there are fewest of; when kinds tie, any of them is right. When only one kind is left, it is the fewest.

What happens when I tap the wrong fruit?

Each level gives three lives. A wrong tap costs one and pauses taps briefly; when no lives are left the level fails and you can retry at once. A shield blocks one wrong tap. Fruit that fall off the screen come back at the top at no cost; a level is cleared when every fruit is gone.

What are skill fruit?

Skill fruit glow with a colored halo. Tapping one is never a mistake, and missing one costs nothing. Levels 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 unlock the lightning grape (shows the fewest fruit), blast pomegranate (clears nearby fruit), ice watermelon (slows the fruit), heart strawberry (restores lives) and meteor starfruit (clears the fewest fruit for you). After that, one skill levels up every ten levels. Every tenth level is a skill party where skill fruit keep coming.

Why is the fruit sometimes faster or slower?

Each level has a fixed starting speed, and the fruit speed up as you clear them. After 20 games, the game also looks at your last 20 results and your current streak: winning makes it a little faster, a run of losses a little slower. It changes only a little each game, never suddenly.

Can I go back to an earlier level?

Yes. Tap Levels on the start, pause or result page, or go to Settings → Choose level, and pick any level you have reached.

How do I start over? Does progress sync to another device?

Settings → Clear progress deletes all progress and results and starts again from the lesson; this cannot be undone. Progress stays on the device with no cross-device sync. Deleting the app loses progress; whether a system backup can restore it depends on your device settings.

How do I report a problem?

Email the game version (at the bottom of Settings), your device model and system version, the level, and the steps to reproduce the problem. You may attach a screenshot without sensitive information.
