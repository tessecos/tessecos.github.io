PRIVACY隐私

隐私政策

更新日期：2026-11-21 · 适用于《最少先爆》（Fewest Pop） 1.0.0

无需创建游戏账号，核心玩法可离线运行。iOS 版的广告、购买和恢复购买需要联网。

进度和设置只保存在当前设备，不向开发者上传，也不通过云端同步。

iOS 版包含 Google AdMob 广告与一次性去广告内购。广告 SDK 会按下文说明处理必要的数据；关卡进度保存在本机。

1. 开发团队与适用范围

本政策说明由 Tessecos Studio 独立开发团队维护的《最少先爆》（Fewest Pop）游戏及本网站如何处理数据。隐私问题可通过下方支持邮箱联系。

2. 设备中的游戏数据

以下内容保存在应用的本地目录：关卡进度和已到达的最高关卡、分数、每一关生成的水果种类与数量、游玩结果（是否通关、失误次数、用时、技能使用次数）、最近对局的胜负记录（用于调整水果下落速度）、技能等级，以及音效开关。这些数据只用于在本机继续游戏和调整难度，开发者无法远程读取。

3. 网络、广告与权限

iOS 版使用 Google Mobile Ads SDK（AdMob）展示横幅、关卡间插屏以及玩家自愿观看的激励视频，并使用 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）管理适用地区的广告隐私选择。广告只在成功加载并可展示时出现；未获得所需隐私状态、网络不可用或没有可用广告时，正常游戏仍可继续。

根据 Google 的 SDK 数据披露，广告及隐私 SDK 可能处理 IP 地址（用于推断大致位置）、设备或应用标识符、广告与产品交互、性能、崩溃和其他诊断数据，用于广告展示与衡量、反作弊、分析以及隐私流程。本版本请求非个性化广告并关闭 Publisher first-party ID；这些设置不等于广告服务完全不处理数据。SDK 的实际处理还取决于地区、系统权限和隐私选择。详情见 Google SDK 数据披露及 Google 隐私政策。

游戏不请求相机、麦克风、精确定位、通讯录或存储卡权限，也不要求提供姓名、生日或游戏账号。本版本不请求跨 App 追踪授权。支持的手机可能提供轻微震动反馈。

内购与恢复购买

iOS 版提供非消耗型商品“去广告”（fewestpop.remove_ads），价格以 App Store 显示的本地价格为准。购买可永久移除广告；符合游戏规则的续命无需观看视频。Apple 负责支付及 App Store 交易处理，开发者不会接收银行卡资料。应用通过 StoreKit 查询商品及购买权益，并在本机缓存购买状态。可在“设置 → 去广告 → 恢复购买”中恢复同一 Apple 账号的购买；恢复购买需要联网。

4. 儿童

游戏无需提供姓名、生日或账号信息。家长若对儿童使用本游戏或广告服务的数据处理有疑问，可以通过支持邮箱联系我们，并参考 Google 的隐私政策与设备隐私设置。

5. 保存与删除

进度只保存在本机，没有跨设备存档同步。你可以在游戏“设置 → 清除进度”中删除全部进度与成绩（音效设置保留），也可以通过删除应用或在系统中清除应用数据来删除所有数据。系统备份是否会包含这些数据取决于你的设备设置。

6. 支持邮件

如果你主动发送支持邮件，运营者会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容及你主动附上的截图或设备信息，仅用于回复和处理请求。请勿发送密码或其他不必要的敏感信息。支持邮件仅在处理问题及适用要求所需的期间保留；你可以通过支持邮箱提出访问或删除请求。

7. 本网站与外部链接

本网站是静态页面，没有广告、分析脚本、登录系统或在线表单。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管；GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者 IP 等技术信息，具体请参阅 GitHub Pages 数据说明和 GitHub 隐私声明。

访问应用商店、GitHub 等外部网站时，相应网站和浏览器会按各自政策处理访问数据。

8. 政策更新

若应用的数据处理方式发生变化，将更新本政策及必要的商店隐私说明，并标明更新日期。

Privacy policy

Updated: 2026-11-21 · Applies to Fewest Pop 1.0.0

No game account is required. Core gameplay works offline. Ads, purchases and restoring purchases in the iOS version require an internet connection.

Progress and settings stay on the current device and are neither uploaded to the developer nor synced through the cloud.

The iOS version includes Google AdMob ads and a one-time Remove Ads purchase. The advertising SDK processes data as described below. Level progress stays on your device.

1. Developer team and scope

This policy describes data handling for Fewest Pop and this website, maintained by the independent team Tessecos Studio. Contact the support email below for privacy questions.

2. Local game data

The following stays in the app’s local directory: level progress and the highest level reached, scores, the fruit kinds and amounts generated for each level, play results (cleared or not, mistakes, time, skill uses), win/loss results of recent games (used to adjust the fall speed), skill levels, and the sound setting. This data is only used to continue playing and adjust difficulty on your device. The developer cannot remotely read it.

3. Network, ads and permissions

The iOS version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for banners, interstitials at level boundaries, and rewarded videos that players may choose to watch. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages ad privacy choices where applicable. Ads appear only after they load and can be shown. If the required privacy status is unavailable, the network is unavailable, or no ad is available, normal gameplay can continue.

Google's SDK disclosures describe possible processing of IP addresses (used to infer approximate location), device or app identifiers, advertising and product interactions, performance, crash and other diagnostic data for ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, analytics and privacy functionality. This version requests non-personalized ads and disables Publisher first-party ID; these settings do not mean the advertising service processes no data. Actual processing also depends on region, system permissions and privacy choices. See Google SDK data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

The game does not request camera, microphone, precise location, contacts or storage access, and does not require a name, date of birth or game account. This version does not request cross-app tracking authorization. Supported phones may provide brief vibration feedback.

Purchases and restoration

The iOS version offers the non-consumable Remove Ads product (fewestpop.remove_ads), at the local price displayed by the App Store. It permanently removes ads and allows eligible revives without a video. Apple handles payment and App Store transactions; the developer does not receive card details. The app uses StoreKit to query product and purchase entitlements and caches purchase state on the device. Restore the same Apple account's purchase in Settings → Remove Ads → Restore Purchases. Restoration requires an internet connection.

4. Children

The game does not require a name, date of birth or game account. Parents with questions about a child using the game or data processed by advertising services can contact support and review Google’s privacy policy and device privacy settings.

5. Saves and deletion

Progress stays on the device, with no cross-device save sync. You can delete all progress and results in Settings → Clear progress (the sound setting is kept), or delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the system settings. Whether system backups include this data depends on your device settings.

6. Support email

If you email support, the operator receives your email address, message and any screenshots or device information you choose to include, solely to respond and handle your request. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Support messages are kept only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable requirements. Contact support to request access or deletion.

7. This website and external links

This is a static website with no ads, analytics scripts, sign-in system or web forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses and technical information for security; see GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you visit app stores, GitHub or other external websites, those websites and your browser process visits under their own policies.

8. Changes

If data handling changes, this policy and necessary store privacy disclosures will be updated, with a revised date.
