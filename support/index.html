玩家支持

棋子为什么不能移动？

只有横向连续至少三颗同色棋子组成整组。单颗或两颗移动后，必须立即横向成组；困难模式下，主动移动的棋子不能越界。教学关须按当前提示的棋子组与方向操作。

炸弹的 3×3 范围如何确定？

在棋盘中间，以点选棋子为中心。靠边时，完整 3×3 区域向棋盘内平移：最下行的棋子位于区域底边中间，角落棋子位于相应角落。只随机打散这九格，颜色数量不变。

如何切换语言？

从菜单打开“设置”，选择“语言”。可选择“跟随系统”或手动选定语言，手动选择会保存到设备。

购买了“全部皮肤 + 去广告”，如何恢复？

在游戏商店点击“恢复购买”，使用原来购买时的 Apple 账号核验“全部皮肤 + 去广告”权益。该商品解锁全部付费皮肤并去除广告，不含关卡解锁。皮肤也可单独购买，仅解锁对应外观；单套商品同样支持恢复购买。

如何获得道具与去广告？

观看礼包广告，已解锁的冰冻和飞行各得 1 个，其余已解锁道具各得 3 个；观看单项广告，冰冻或飞行得 3 个，其余技能得 10 个。两种奖励均仅当前一局有效，重开或换关失效。一次性购买“全部皮肤 + 去广告”后，广告按钮消失，已解锁道具无限使用，下方显示 ∞。通过 Google AdMob 播放激励广告；广告不可用或未获得 SDK 领奖确认时不发奖励。

更换设备后，进度会自动同步吗？

进度保存在当前设备，没有跨设备存档同步。系统备份恢复取决于设备设置。“全部皮肤 + 去广告”可用原购买 Apple 账号点击“恢复购买”核验；本机对局与道具不会由此恢复。

如何反馈故障？

请通过下方邮箱说明游戏版本、iOS版本、设备型号、难度与关卡，以及复现步骤。可以附上不含敏感内容的截图。无需提供 Apple 密码或付款账号。

Player support

Why can’t a tile move?

Only horizontal runs of at least three matching tiles form a group. A single tile or pair must form a horizontal group immediately after moving. In Hard mode, active tiles cannot cross the edge. Tutorial levels require the instructed group and direction.

How is the Bomb’s 3×3 area chosen?

In the middle of the board, the chosen tile is the center. At an edge, the whole 3×3 area shifts inward: a bottom-edge tile is at the bottom-middle, and a corner tile is at the matching corner. Only these nine tiles are shuffled; color counts do not change.

How do I change language?

Open Settings from the menu and choose Language. Use Follow System or select a language manually; your choice is saved on the device.

How do I restore All Skins + No Ads?

Tap Restore Purchases in the game’s shop using the Apple Account that made the purchase. The bundle unlocks every paid appearance and removes ads; it does not unlock levels. Skins are also sold individually, granting only that appearance. Individual purchases can be restored too.

How do tools and ad removal work?

The bundle ad gives one Freeze and one Flight plus three of every other unlocked tool. Individual ads give three Freeze or Flight, or ten of another tool. Only unlocked tools are awarded. Both rewards last only for the current run and expire on restart or level change. The one-time All Skins + No Ads purchase removes the ad buttons and gives unlimited uses of unlocked tools, marked ∞. Google AdMob provides the rewarded video. Unavailable ads or ads without an SDK-earned reward grant nothing.

Does progress sync to another device?

Progress stays on the device, with no cross-device save sync. System backup recovery depends on your device settings. Restore Purchases verifies All Skins + No Ads with the original purchasing Apple Account; it does not recover local runs or tools.

How do I report a problem?

Email the game version, iOS version, device model, mode and level, and steps to reproduce the problem. You may attach a screenshot without sensitive information. Do not send Apple passwords or payment-account details.
