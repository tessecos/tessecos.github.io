玩家支持

棋子为什么不能移动？

只有横向连续至少三颗同色棋子组成整组。单颗或两颗移动后，必须立即横向成组；困难模式下，主动移动的棋子不能越界。教学关须按当前提示的棋子组与方向操作。无法移动时会弹窗说明原因。

如何切换语言？

从菜单打开“设置”，选择“语言”。可选择“跟随系统”或手动选定语言，手动选择会保存到设备。

购买了皮肤包，如何恢复？

在游戏商店点击“恢复购买”，使用原来购买时的 Apple 账号核验全部皮肤包权益。皮肤包只解锁外观，不含道具、关卡或去广告权益。

如何获得道具与去广告？

主动看完一次激励广告可获得所有已解锁道具各一个，也可选择单个道具的广告按钮获得一个。两种奖励均仅当前一局有效，重开或换关失效。一次性购买去广告后，已解锁道具无限使用，下方显示 ∞。通过 Google AdMob 播放激励广告；广告不可用或未获得 SDK 领奖确认时不发奖励。

更换设备后，进度会自动同步吗？

进度保存在当前设备，没有跨设备存档同步。系统备份恢复取决于设备设置。皮肤包与去广告可用原购买 Apple 账号点击“恢复购买”核验；本机对局与道具不会由此恢复。

如何反馈故障？

请通过下方邮箱说明游戏版本、iOS版本、设备型号、难度与关卡，以及复现步骤。可以附上不含敏感内容的截图。无需提供 Apple 密码或付款账号。

Player support

Why can’t a tile move?

Only horizontal runs of at least three matching tiles form a group. A single tile or pair must form a horizontal group immediately after moving. In Hard mode, active tiles cannot cross the edge. Tutorial levels require the instructed group and direction. Rejected moves show an explanation.

How do I change language?

Open Settings from the menu and choose Language. Use Follow System or select a language manually; your choice is saved on the device.

How do I restore the skin pack?

Tap Restore Purchases in the game’s shop using the Apple Account that made the purchase. The All Skins Pack unlocks appearances only. Tools, levels and Remove Ads are separate.

How do tools and Remove Ads work?

Complete one optional rewarded ad for one of every unlocked tool, or use an individual tool’s ad button for one of that tool. Both rewards last only for the current run and expire on restart or level change. The one-time Remove Ads purchase gives unlimited uses of unlocked tools, marked ∞. Google AdMob provides the rewarded video. Unavailable ads or ads without an SDK-earned reward grant nothing.

Does progress sync to another device?

Progress stays on the device, with no cross-device save sync. System backup recovery depends on your device settings. Restore Purchases verifies the skin pack and Remove Ads with the original purchasing Apple Account; it does not recover local runs or tools.

How do I report a problem?

Email the game version, iOS version, device model, mode and level, and steps to reproduce the problem. You may attach a screenshot without sensitive information. Do not send Apple passwords or payment-account details.
